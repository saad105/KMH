WELCOME TO KMH Engineering

KMH Engineering is established to offer a wide range of best quality and technically competent structural engineering services. The KMH team, in close association with the client, seeks to provide the best quality services with time bound deliveries. Specialized in "Fast Track" projects, we have a flexible approach to the particular requirements of each project and can tailor the services offered accordingly. With our design management experience gained over years, we provide a specialist, practical solutions to the discerning projects.We have delivered some of the most enduring, high profile structures in various regions of the world such as Dubai, Abudhabi, Oman, Hongkong, Jordan & India. Several large and medium projects have been delivered in cities like Mumbai, Delhi, Ahmedabad, Hyderabad, and Bangalore in India.
